HOW TO PLAY

TAP TO LAUNCH on the pad (or press Space) — one tap, confirm flash, then go. After liftoff, climb stays on. Just steer: drag left/right on a phone, or A D / optional ◀ ▶ on a keyboard. Stay between the dotted CORRIDOR rails — those are the fly-out bounds, not decoration. Grab the cyan aero shield when you can. Watch the INT bar — grazes hurt, a lethal hit is a RUD. Through MAX-Q, fly smooth. After MECO you are in space. Stay in the marked SEP ZONE, ALIGN green, then press SEPARATE. Haven is a long strake glide: follow the PAINT mark on the Haven deck, wait for the burn window, then HOLD TO BURN and keep holding while you steer onto the paint. Soft deck = BOOSTER RECOVERED. Tip, hard catch, or splash clips score — not a wipe. A RUD ends the flight and names the reason. Hangar unlocks parts as you fly. Each KH-n has its own time of day. The bottom tape is the roadmap: NOW is this beat, NEXT is the one after. KID / CADET / PAD RAT change the margin. P pauses. M mutes. O opens settings.
