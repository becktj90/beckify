Image Intake
Privacy before you pick a photo. The photo stays on this device. It is never uploaded to Beckify or any server unless you turn on Enhance with AI and then click Read Schedule. On-device Tesseract.js is the default. The image is not saved after you leave or reset.

No custom URL yet. If the Beckify API base is injected at deploy, that proxy will be used. Otherwise Read Schedule stays on-device.

Photograph the directory / label only. This tool reads the printed circuit directory or schedule card on the panel door — not the live interior of an open panel. Keep the door closed if you can. Never open a panel or touch anything inside it to get this photo. Square the card to the camera, fill the frame, use even light, and avoid glare.
