# Kestrel Heavy privacy

Standalone app (`com.beckify.kestrelheavy`). Public URL for App Store Connect: https://beckify.com/privacy

## What leaves the device

**Nothing** in v1. The Phaser cabinet loads from the app bundle. Scores and settings stay in on-device web storage.

## What does not happen

- No account
- No ads, analytics, or tracking
- No camera, microphone, or location
- No upload of play data
- The app does not load beckify.com in the web view

Play is entertainment. Fictional launch arcade — not a simulator or a licensed vehicle.
