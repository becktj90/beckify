## App privacy (nutrition label)

Data collection: **Photos** only when the user taps **Analyze**. Not linked to identity. Not used for tracking.

- No analytics
- No tracking
- No advertising identifier
- No account
- No Saved Jobs / UserDefaults product storage
- Camera and photo library picker stay on device until Analyze

Privacy manifest: `LookCheck/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`  
- `NSPrivacyTracking` = false  
- Photos or Videos collected for App Functionality, not linked, not used for tracking

Usage strings (generated Info.plist via `INFOPLIST_KEY_*` on the LookCheck target):

- `NSCameraUsageDescription` — Look Check uses the camera to take a photo for a look verdict and roast. The photo stays on this device until you tap Analyze.
- Photo Library full access is not requested; the system picker is used.

## Export compliance

HTTPS only: `https://api.beckify.com/api/analyze-look`. No custom cryptography. Info.plist includes `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO`. In App Store Connect, answer **No** to “Does your app use encryption?” except the standard HTTPS exemption if the form still appears.
